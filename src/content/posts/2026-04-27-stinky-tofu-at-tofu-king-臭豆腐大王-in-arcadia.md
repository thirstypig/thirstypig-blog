Stinky tofu at Tofu King 臭豆腐大王 in Arcadia.  Taiwanese food of the home style variety. Along with the stinky fried tofu, we had the sliced beef (served cold which is a traditional Taiwanese appetizer), beef noodle soup, rice noodle soup, a Rou yuan (a meat ball with rice). All was pretty good.  Problem is.  They close at 7 and they accept cash or Zelle - not a fan of.  I’d try again. . . #taiwanesefood #sgv #arcadiaeats
