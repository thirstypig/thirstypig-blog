# SEO Audit — thirstypig.com (2026-06-03)

Full-corpus scan (not a sample). Scanned `src/content/posts/*.md`: **2,127 markdown files** →
**1,639 published** (indexed) + **482 drafts** (excluded from pages & sitemap via `!p.data.draft`)
+ **6 unparseable frontmatter** (see §1.1). All percentages below are over the **1,639 published** posts,
since drafts aren't indexed.

> **Schema note:** the repo uses `pubDate` (not `date`) and `heroImage` (not `image`). `title` and
> `pubDate` are **schema-required** (`z.string()`, `z.coerce.date()`), so no published post can be
> missing them — the build would fail. So "missing title/date" counts are 0 by construction.

## 1.4 SEO.astro Component

Read `src/components/SEO.astro`. **Infrastructure is solid:**

- ✅ `canonical` — `new URL(Astro.url.pathname, siteBase)` (line 34/112)
- ✅ `og:title` / `og:description` / `og:type` — always populated
- ⚠️ `og:image` — **only rendered when `heroImage` exists** (line 119). 38 posts have no hero → no `og:image`.
- ✅ `twitter:card` — `summary_large_image` when image present, else `summary`
- ✅ `article:published_time` — `publishDate.toISOString()` (line 130)
- ✅ Restaurant JSON-LD fires when `location && (address || coordinates)` (line 44)
- ✅ Article JSON-LD fires for every post page (`type==='article' && publishDate`)
- ✅ BreadcrumbList (when a category exists) + WebSite/SearchAction (homepage)
- ⚠️ **`description` has a hard-coded default** (line 21). So the 423 no-description posts all emit the
  **same identical `<meta description>` / `og:description`** — a *duplicate-meta-description* signal across
  423 pages, which is worse than empty. This is the most fixable high-impact item.

## 1.7 Image SEO

| Check | Count | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| No hero image (→ no `og:image`) | 38 | real gap (small) |
| Body `![]()` images missing alt | 165 imgs across 20 posts | minor |
| Hero `heroImage` not `.webp` | 1,601 | **NOT an issue — by design** |

> The 1,601 "non-webp heroes" are expected: `heroImage` stores the `.jpg` path; the image pipeline serves
> WebP via a sibling `<picture><source>`. `og:image` *should* be JPG/PNG (social crawlers don't reliably
> support WebP), so JPG in `heroImage` is correct. Flagging so this isn't mistaken for a regression.

## 1.8 Heading Structure (random sample of 50 published)

- Multiple H1s: **0** — the layout renders the title as the single H1; no markdown `#` H1s in bodies ✓
- Longer posts (>150 words) with no subheadings: 13/50 (~26%) — minor; IG-caption posts are short prose

## 1.9 Performance / CWV Signals

- **External scripts** (`BaseLayout.astro`): GA4 + AdSense are **consent-gated** (`type="text/plain"`,
  inert pre-consent); Vercel Analytics + CookieConsent are **bundled** (self-hosted). Maps loads only on
  map/location pages. No always-on render-blocking third-party JS.
- **CLS**: the remark image pipeline emits explicit `width`/`height` on `<img>`; `AdSlot` reserves
  `min-height`. Low CLS risk.
- ⚠️ **LCP nuance**: the post hero uses `loading="lazy" fetchpriority="low"`. That's correct *because*
  the layout renders caption text above the hero (hero is usually below the fold). On short posts where
  the hero is the LCP element, lazy/low-priority could delay LCP — worth a Lighthouse spot-check, not a
  blanket change.

## 6 unparseable-frontmatter posts (PyYAML rejects; build accepts)

`canoodle-shanghai`, `pantry-by-the-co-op-shanghai`, `the-farmhouse-caffe-shanghai`,
`southern-barbarian-shanghai`, `xibo-shanghai`, `jun-yue-pork-chop-taipei` (all 2012). They have a
unicode quoting quirk PyYAML's scanner rejects but `js-yaml`/the build tolerates — they render fine.
Worth a manual look (potential malformed quoting), but **not build-breaking**.

# Phase 2 — Prioritized Remediation Plan

**No P0 (nothing broken):** canonical, sitemap, robots, JSON-LD, and the build are all sound.

| Priority | Issue | Affected | Complexity | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | 423 posts share ONE identical fallback meta/og description (duplicate-description signal) | 423 | Med | High |
| **P1** | 423 generic "Instagram Post …" titles (non-descriptive in SERP) | 423 | Med | High |
| **P1** | 38 posts with no `heroImage` → no `og:image` (poor social cards) | 38 | Low | Med |
| **P2** | 808 descriptions > 160 chars (SERP truncation) | 808 | Med | Med |
| **P2** | 199 duplicate titles across 80 venues (repeat visits compete) | 199 | Med | Med |
| **P2** | 431 venue posts lack address/coords → no Restaurant rich result | 431 | High | Med |
| **P2** | 205 titles < 20 chars; 87 thin descriptions | ~290 | Med | Low-Med |
| **P3** | 165 body images missing `alt` (20 posts) | 20 | Low | Low |
| **P3** | 6 posts with unicode frontmatter quoting quirk | 6 | Low | Low |
| **P3** | 13/50 longer posts without subheadings | ~ | Low | Low |

## Suggested sequencing
1. **P1 — descriptions:** the 423 no-description posts are the highest-leverage, lowest-risk win
   (kill the duplicate-description signal). Decide: generate per-post descriptions from caption/body, OR
   `noindex` the truly content-less ones.
2. **P1 — titles:** same 423 set — derive a real title from caption/venue, or noindex.
3. **P1 — og:image:** backfill heroes (or a default share image) for the 38.
4. **P2 — trim long descriptions** to ~155 chars (deterministic, no AI needed).
5. **P2 — duplicate titles:** disambiguate repeat-venue titles with date or dish.
6. **P2 — Restaurant schema:** geocode the 431 (Google Places, not Foursquare) to unlock rich results.

> **Open decision before any P1 work:** are the 423 generic IG photo-posts worth enriching for SEO, or
> should they be `noindex`/`draft`? That choice determines whether P1 is "write descriptions" or
> "de-index thin content." Recommend deciding this first.
